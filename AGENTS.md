## Current product rules

- **Branch boundary:** Muscat staff see only Muscat work; Barka staff see only Barka work, including properties, clients/requests, conversations, and related private data. Owner sees every branch. Do not reintroduce company-wide reads through a view, RPC, Edge Function, export, search, or cached UI state. Branch route is authoritative for WhatsApp visibility even if a stale `assigned_to` points elsewhere. Manager access follows explicit role policy; verify before broadening it.
- A property visit creates or maintains the client/property interest in the same transaction. A visit-derived interest is marked `has_inbound_inquiry=false`; do not count it as a proven inbound WhatsApp inquiry. Cancelled visits do not create one. Funnel numerators/denominators and owner reports must use the appropriate real records, not status labels as proxy visits.

Ambiguous references require staff review/alert; do not silently attach an arbitrary property. Existing historic ambiguity remains review work. AI attribution does not send a client reply.
- Opening a conversation in CRM marks its messages read **inside CRM** after loading. Do not claim to change the WhatsApp phone app's read receipts. Staff must not see the other branch's inbox. Keep operator review state useful.
- Employee performance supports day, Oman-local Sunday–Saturday week, month, and company inception through today (not a yearly substitute). The approved standing active-inventory target is 10 per employee: existing active properties count regardless of acquisition month. New-properties targets count additions during the selected period and are separately configured by the owner, as are inquiries, completed visits, sales and company commission targets. Show sourced active/new listings, proven property inquiries, inquiry→visit, booked/completed visits, visit→sale, sales, human-documented work, and staff outbound activity. Show a percentage only over positive configured targets and label coverage; the standing active-inventory 10 is explicitly approved, while other unconfigured targets are excluded. Explain configured-target coverage in words, never an ambiguous 1/5 label. Do not compare lifetime totals to a single month's goals. Each agent may see company commission from her own attributed work in the allowed branch only; do not reveal the other branch or broaden access to other financial fields.
