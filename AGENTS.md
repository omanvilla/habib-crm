## Current product rules

- **Branch boundary:** Muscat staff see only Muscat work; Barka staff see only Barka work, including properties, clients/requests, conversations, and related private data. Owner sees every branch. Do not reintroduce company-wide reads through a view, RPC, Edge Function, export, search, or cached UI state. Branch route is authoritative for WhatsApp visibility even if a stale `assigned_to` points elsewhere. Manager access follows explicit role policy; verify before broadening it.
- A property visit creates or maintains the client/property interest in the same transaction. A visit-derived interest is marked `has_inbound_inquiry=false`; do not count it as a proven inbound WhatsApp inquiry. Cancelled visits do not create one. Funnel numerators/denominators and owner reports must use the appropriate real records, not status labels as proxy visits.
- Inbound WhatsApp references to a specific listing may be attributed automatically only on a high-confidence AI result corroborated by an exact code/title or unique area plus price. Ambiguous references require staff review/alert; do not silently attach an arbitrary property. Existing historic ambiguity remains review work. AI attribution does not send a client reply.
- Opening a conversation in CRM marks its messages read **inside CRM** after loading. Do not claim to change the WhatsApp phone app's read receipts. Staff must not see the other branch's inbox. Keep operator review state useful. During read-only live audits, inspect inbox lists without opening real threads or changing their read state.
- Employee performance supports day, Oman-local Sunday–Saturday week, month, and company inception through today (not a yearly substitute). The approved standing active-inventory target is 10 per employee: existing active properties count regardless of acquisition month. The monthly acquisition requirement is automatically max(0,10-current_active_inventory), not a separate owner-entered new-properties goal. Show its reminder directly on the employee dashboard on every visit while short, hide it at 10 or more, and restore it if active inventory drops. Monthly new-property additions remain a descriptive metric; do not score a second target for the same inventory shortfall. Inquiries, completed visits, sales and company commission goals remain owner-configured. Show sourced active/new listings, proven property inquiries, inquiry→visit, booked/completed visits, visit→sale, sales, human-documented work, and staff outbound activity. Owner-approved attribution effective 2026-08-01 credits Muscat branch work to active Hadeel and Barka branch work to active Maram for reporting. Assign existing and future classified properties to those employees for active inventory, irrespective of listing month. Preserve original record dates, creators, senders, deal amounts and historical records; keep previous property attribution in a private audit. Do not guess unclassified branches. Show a percentage only over positive configured targets and label coverage; the standing active-inventory 10 is explicitly approved, while other unconfigured targets are excluded. Explain configured-target coverage in words, never an ambiguous 1/5 label. Do not compare lifetime totals to a single month's goals. Owner must see company commission attributable to each employee. Employee-facing visibility is for her own documented work and commission only, never other employees or the other branch; a company-commission row for employees is not a substitute for the owner's report. Employee commission is a per-deal owner-selected percentage of the company's commission, never the property sale price, and may vary from deal to deal. Preserve existing percentages/amounts until an explicit deal-level change; never infer a new percentage or retroactively recalculate history. A week spanning months must not show a target percentage until the goal allocation rule is approved. A zero-sale month can still show documented productive work. This is an operational score, not a compensation formula.

Property browsing is organized by branch, area, and newest first; owner clients have branch filters; phone numbers must display left-to-right correctly in Arabic layouts.
