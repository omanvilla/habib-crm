## Current product rules

- **Branch boundary:** Muscat staff see only Muscat work; Barka staff see only Barka work, including properties, clients/requests, conversations, and related private data. Owner sees every branch. Do not reintroduce company-wide reads through a view, RPC, Edge Function, export, search, or cached UI state. Branch route is authoritative for WhatsApp visibility even if a stale `assigned_to` points elsewhere. Manager access follows explicit role policy; verify before broadening it.
- A property visit creates or maintains the client/property interest in the same transaction. A visit-derived interest is marked `has_inbound_inquiry=false`; do not count it as a proven inbound WhatsApp inquiry. Cancelled visits do not create one. Funnel numerators/denominators and owner reports must use the appropriate real records, not status labels as proxy visits.
- Inbound WhatsApp references to a specific listing may be attributed automatically only on a high-confidence AI result corroborated by an exact code/title or unique area plus price. Ambiguous references require staff review/alert; do not silently attach an arbitrary property. Existing historic ambiguity remains review work. AI attribution does not send a client reply.
- Opening a conversation in CRM marks its messages read **inside CRM** after loading. Do not claim to change the WhatsApp phone app's read receipts. Staff must not see the other branch's inbox. Keep operator review state useful.
- Employee performance supports day, Oman-local Sunday–Saturday week, and month. Show sourced active/new listings, proven property inquiries, inquiry→visit, booked/completed visits, visit→sale, sales, human-documented work, and staff outbound activity. Attribute work to a person only with evidence, not a guess based on branch. Show a percentage only over positive configured targets and label coverage; the inventory default currently displayed is 10. A zero-sale month can still show documented productive work. This is an operational score, not a compensation formula.
- Owner reports distinguish actual scheduled/completed/cancelled visits, proven inbound inquiries, and interest inferred from visits. Missing Instagram metrics mean unavailable, not zero. Property browsing is organized by branch, area, and newest first; owner clients have branch filters; phone numbers must display left-to-right correctly in Arabic layouts.
- Avoid noisy automatic follow-ups. The previously requested hourly Meta follow-up and other scheduled automations were observed disabled on 2026-09-30; do not reactivate them without a specific new request. Investigate usage concerns from actual data rather than assuming a visible “working for two days” indicator is an active automation.
